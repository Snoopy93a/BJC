About us

Read, understand, reproduce and innovate.

The Bioinformatics Journal Club is a collaborative space for learning, reflection and scientific practice dedicated to bioinformatics.

Our mission: to build in every participant a genuine capacity for bioinformatic reasoning, along with autonomy and critical thinking.

Bioinformatics is not just running software. It is a particular way of thinking about biological problems. The BJC is not a programming course or a simple reading group: it is a scientific community.

Our pillars

Scientific Reading

Reading and understanding publications: questions, hypotheses, methods, results and limits.

Computational Practice

Handling biological data, discovering tools, programming and carrying out analyses.

Critical Thinking

Evaluating methodologies, interpreting results, spotting bias and reasoning scientifically.

Mini Research Project

Progressively carrying out a mini research project that uses what the club has taught.
